Privacy Policy

SciPad Scientific Calculator 991 (Android app, package com.scipad.calculator)
Effective 29 October 2026

In short: the app collects no personal data, has no ads, no accounts and no analytics, and does not connect to the internet. Everything you type stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer of SciPad does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any information about you or your use of the app.

What the app keeps on your phone

To work as a calculator, the app saves a few things in its own private storage on your device only:

  • your calculation history (up to the last 300 calculations, with the time of each)
  • your settings, such as theme, sound, vibration, angle unit and number format
  • values you store in variables or in custom functions you define

This information never leaves your phone through the app. You can delete the history from the History screen. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, removes all of it.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used only for the short buzz when you press a key. You can switch it off in the app's Settings.

The app does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or any other permission.

Internet, ads and third parties

The app works completely offline. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no crash-reporting service and no sign-in. No third party receives any data from the app.

Copying results

When you tap copy, the result is placed on your phone's clipboard at your request. The app does not read the clipboard.

Device backups

Android may include an app's saved data (such as your history and settings) in your phone's own backup to your Google account if you have backups turned on. That is controlled by your phone and your Google account, not by us, and we cannot see it.

Children

The app is a general-purpose calculator. Because it collects no information at all, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/KuchKehNahiSakte/scipad-privacy/issues.
